# Internationalization (i18next)

i18n is part of the frontend foundation — follow the established `i18next` +
`react-i18next` setup. (Use the `i18n-add-string` skill to add a key.)

- Locales live in `apps/web/src/lib/i18n/locales/`: **`en`** and **`es`** are
  production; **`en-XA`** is a generated pseudo-locale.
- Namespaces: `common` (UI chrome, labels) and `app` (domain strings). Put a new
  key in the right namespace and keep `en` + `es` in sync.
- **Pseudo-locale (`en-XA`) is intentional QA tooling** — generated from English
  resources for dev/test layout, overflow, interpolation, and hard-coded-string
  review. Keep it available; the implementation may improve over time, but do
  **not** remove it when production locales are added, and do **not** expose
  pseudo-locales as user-selectable production locales.
- Locale-aware formatting (currency, dates, numbers) goes through
  `apps/web/src/lib/formatting/`; use date-fns v4 for dates.
